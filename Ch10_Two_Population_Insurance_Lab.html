Do Northeast and Southwest customers cost the same?

Ha: μNE − μSW ≠ 0

The twist

Bridger’s full records show the Northeast really does average $1,059 more. So H0 is false, and we did not reject it. Which error is that?

Type II. The difference is real, but $1,059 is small next to σ ≈ $11,000. Slide n to see how big an audit would need to be.

Excel: =COUNTIFS(insurance!B:B,"male",insurance!E:E,"yes")

Why pool for the test, but not for the interval?

The test assumes H0 is true: one smoking rate for everyone. Our best estimate of that single rate combines both samples: the pooled p̄.

The interval doesn’t assume the rates are equal. It estimates how far apart they are, so each group keeps its own p̄.

Men are 2 to 10 percentage points more likely to smoke. Connect it back to Stop 4: is that part of why some regions cost more?
