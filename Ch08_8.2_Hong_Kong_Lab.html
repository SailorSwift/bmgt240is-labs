More degrees of freedom → less spread → closer to z. Past 100 df, z is a good approximation.

t distribution

A family of probability distributions that can be used to develop an interval estimate of a population mean whenever σ is unknown and is estimated by the sample standard deviation s.

Textbook Ch 8 key terms

Degrees of freedom

A parameter of the t distribution. For an interval estimate of a population mean, the t distribution has n − 1 degrees of freedom.
