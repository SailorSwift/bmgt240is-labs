Textbook Ch 8 key terms

Confidence level
95%

The confidence associated with an interval estimate. If a procedure gives intervals such that 95% of them include the population parameter, the interval estimate is constructed at the 95% confidence level.

Textbook Ch 8 key terms

Confidence coefficient · 1 − α
0.95

The confidence level expressed as a decimal value. For example, 0.95 is the confidence coefficient for a 95% confidence level.

Textbook Ch 8 key terms

Level of significance · α
0.05

STOP 6 · APPLY

Solve the Houston problem in six steps

Computed from houston.xlsx, A2:A65, with σ = $6 at 99% confidence.

Say it right

Right

We are 99% confident that the mean lunch bill of all customers is between $19.59 and $23.45.

Wrong

99% of customers spend between $19.59 and $23.45.

The interval is about the mean, not individual bills. The 64 bills run from $6.83 to $36.55.
